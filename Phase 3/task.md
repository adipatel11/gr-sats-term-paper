Phase 3: Research Poster Presentation Submission
Phase 3 focuses on presenting your research progress, methodology, experimental findings, and contributions through a single-slide research poster prepared in PowerPoint.

The poster should summarize your research from the problem statement and literature review to the proposed methodology, experimental results, and conclusions.

1. Required Poster Components and Presentation Time Allocation
   Prepare your research poster to support an approximately 15-minute presentation and technical discussion. Use the following data points to be covered in the presentation.

Time Component Required Datapoints
0:00–1:00 Research Overview Research title, team members, research objective, and central research question
1:00–2:00 Problem and Motivation Problem definition, significance, and real-world relevance
2:00–3:00 Literature Review and Research Gaps Key existing studies, three major research gaps, and limitations
3:00–4:00 Research Challenges and Contributions Technical challenges, novelty, and proposed contributions
4:00–6:00 Proposed Methodology Architecture, algorithms, workflow diagram, and explanation of the major components
6:00–7:00 Dataset and Preprocessing Dataset source, size, features, target variables, preprocessing, and data partitioning
7:00–8:00 Experimental Setup Baselines, implementation details, evaluation metrics, and validation strategy
8:00–11:00 Experimental Results Key graphs, tables, performance findings, and interpretation
11:00–12:00 Comparative Analysis Performance comparison with existing methods, improvements, and weaknesses
12:00–13:00 Research Discussion Interpretation of findings, achievement of objectives, and limitations
13:00–14:00 Conclusions and Future Work Major findings, research contributions, and possible improvements
14:00–15:00 Team Contributions and AI Usage Individual contributions, AI tools used, and verification methods 2. Poster Preparation Guidelines
Prepare only one PowerPoint slide formatted as a research poster, not a conventional multi-slide presentation.

Organize the poster into clearly labeled sections covering the required data points.

Use architecture diagrams, methodology flowcharts, comparison tables, and experimental graphs wherever appropriate.

Give particular importance to the proposed methodology, experimental results, and research contributions.

Keep the content concise and visually organized rather than using lengthy paragraphs.

Every graph must include appropriate axis labels, legends, and evaluation metrics.

Ensure consistency between the poster and your ongoing Overleaf research paper.

Present only genuine experimental results. Clearly identify any incomplete experiments.

3. Presentation and Discussion Expectations
   The poster should support approximately 15 minutes of technical presentation and discussion.

Be prepared to explain why your methodology is needed, how it addresses the identified research gaps, why the experimental setup is appropriate, and what the results demonstrate.

Every team member must understand the research and be prepared to explain their individual contributions.

4. Submission Instructions
   Submission: One research poster in PowerPoint (.pptx) format.

Format: Single-slide poster.

Submission location: Phase 3 Canvas Discussions.

Presentation duration: Approximately 15 minutes.
